Questions

Frequently asked

What does a platform like this cost?

It depends on the number of shops, features and whether we start from proven building blocks. After the 2-week planning stage you get a fixed quote, so there are no open-ended bills.

How soon can customers start ordering?

A pilot with your first 5–10 shops is typically live in 8–10 weeks from the start of the build.

Will it work outside the metros, in Jaipur or smaller towns?

Yes. UPI payments, Hindi/English screens and light apps that work on basic phones and slow networks are part of the plan from day one.

Can you run it after launch?

Yes. I can stay on for support and improvements, or train your team and hand over completely. Either way, you own the code and data.

Should we sell on Paytm/Amazon or build our own platform?

Often both. Marketplaces bring reach while your own platform builds loyalty and saves commission. I'll help you choose the mix in the planning stage.
